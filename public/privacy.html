Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Ready Ready Motors, Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates Ready Ready eSign to prepare, send, sign, and manage vehicle and business documents. This policy explains how information is handled through this application, including its use of Google services.

Information we collect

  • Account and contact information: administrator sign-in information and the names and email addresses of saved contacts and document participants.
  • Documents and signatures: uploaded PDFs, documents imported from Google Drive, document titles and contents, signature images, signing dates, field placements, and completed documents. Documents may contain information such as addresses or driver's license details when included by their participants.
  • Signing records: invitation and document identifiers, delivery and viewing events, electronic-signature consent, signer names and email addresses, signing timestamps, signature method, document hashes, and request information such as IP address and user agent recorded with signing events.
  • Google authorization: account identity and OAuth tokens needed for the Google features an authorized administrator connects.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate administrators, prepare and display documents, maintain a recipient address book, send signing invitations and reminders, record consent and signatures, deliver completed PDFs and status notifications, maintain audit records, and protect the service from unauthorized use.

Google account access

  • Gmail sending: an authorized company administrator connects the designated company sending account. We request Gmail sending permission and basic account identity to verify the sender and send invitations, reminders, and document notifications, including completed PDF attachments. This integration does not request permission to read the Gmail inbox.
  • Google Drive import: if an administrator chooses Google Doc import, the app requests read-only Drive access to export the selected accessible Google document as a PDF. That PDF is then stored and processed as an eSign document. Drive read-only permission can permit access beyond the selected file; use a Google account appropriate for the documents being imported.
  • Authorization credentials: the Gmail refresh token and OAuth client secret are held on the backend for continued authorized sending. They are not intentionally exposed to signing recipients. Google access can be revoked in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete documents already imported or signing records already created.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used for the document and communication features described here, not for advertising, sale to data brokers, or training generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Who receives information

Documents and signing information are available to authorized company administrators and the intended document participants as needed for the signing workflow. Participants may receive the completed PDF, which can include other participants' signatures and certificate information. Keep private signing links confidential; a person with a valid link may be able to access its signing session.

We use Supabase for authentication, database, private file storage, and backend processing; GitHub Pages for hosting the web application and this policy; and Google services for connected Google features and email delivery. These providers process information necessary to provide their services under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect legal rights and service security. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and security

The application uses administrator access controls, private document storage, and time-limited signing links. Administrator sessions and limited document-list data may be stored in the browser to support sign-in and navigation. Providers may maintain service and security logs. No online storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Documents, contacts, signing records, and audit information are retained as needed for business operations, agreements, dispute resolution, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Removing a saved contact does not erase their details from existing signing records. Deleting a draft removes it from active app views; it does not immediately purge retained PDFs, prior signing records, or audit history. Browser sign-out does not delete server records.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information, contact us below. We may need to verify your identity and retain information necessary for legal obligations, established agreements, or other legitimate recordkeeping needs. Revoking Google authorization and requesting deletion are separate actions.

Your choices

You can decline electronic signing by closing the signing page and contacting Ready Ready Motors to discuss another method. Administrators can manage saved contacts and revoke connected Google access. Contact us about privacy requests or questions regarding your document.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the application changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
